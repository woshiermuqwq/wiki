## 描述
Modifies the threat level that the caster holds with the target entities


## 属性
| 属性 | 缩写 | 描述 | 默认值 |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| mode      | m         | How the threat 应当 assigned                                    | taunt<!--type:ThreatMode-->|

> 此机制继承所有[threat](/skills/mechanics/threat) 机制


## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - taunt @EIR{r=20}
```
