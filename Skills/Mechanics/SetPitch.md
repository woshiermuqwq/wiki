## 描述
Sets the head 俯仰角(pitch) of the 目标 entity


## 属性
| 属性 | 缩写   | 描述                                                          | 默认值 |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| amount    | a, 俯仰角(pitch), p | The value the 俯仰角(pitch) 将被设为 to                                 | 0       |


## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - setpitch{amount=45} @EIR{r=10}
```
