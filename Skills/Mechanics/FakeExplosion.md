## 描述
Creates an explosion effect at the specified 目标. Will play the sound and the 粒子 effects, but won't cause any damage.


## 属性
> *This 技能 has no attributes*


## 示例
```yaml
ExplosionSkill:
  Skills:
  - fakeexplosion @self
```

- [x] e:explosion
