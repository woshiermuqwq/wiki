## 描述
Removes 生物的 active 伪装, if it has one.  
此技能需要安装 Libs' 伪装 才能启用伪装功能。

See [Extra: 伪装](/生物/伪装) for how to apply and use 伪装.

> **This is a no-目标 技能, and the affected entity will always be the 施法者**

## 属性
> *This 技能 has no attributes*


## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - undisguise ~onInteract
```
> Undisguise the 生物 when it is right clicked.


## 别名
- [x] disguiseRemove
