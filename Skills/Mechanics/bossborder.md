## 描述
Traps the the 目标 player within a vanilla world border around the 施法者.


## 属性
| 属性 | 缩写   | 描述                                                          | 默认值 |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| 半径    | r         | The 半径 of the border                                             | 32      |


## 示例
```yml
  Skills:
  - bossBorder{r=40} @NearestPlayer{r=30}
```


## 别名
- [x] effect:bossBorder
- [x] e:bossBorder
