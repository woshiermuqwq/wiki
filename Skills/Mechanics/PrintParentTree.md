## 描述
Prints debug information regarding the Metaskill executing the 机制 and its SkillTree


## 属性
> *This 机制 has no attributes*


## 示例
```yaml
Example1:
  Skills:
  - skill{s=Example2}

Example2:
  Skills:
  - skill{s=Example3}

Example3:
  Skills:
  - printParentTree
```
