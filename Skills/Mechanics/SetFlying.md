## 描述
Sets whether the 目标 player is flying


## 属性
| 属性 | 缩写   | 描述                                                          | 默认值 |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| flying    | f, state, value | Whether the 目标 player is flying                            | false   |


## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - setflying{f=true} @PIR{r=2}
```
