## 描述
Drops a copy of the target players' head. Does nothing when used
against a non-player entity.


## 属性
> *This 机制 has no attributes*


## 示例
Drops the head of the person who clicked on the mob.
```yaml
  Skills:
  - decapitate @trigger ~onInteract
```


## 别名
- [x] dropHead
