## 描述
Causes the mob to jump off of its mount, if it is mounted on anything.
Otherwise, does nothing. 

> **This is a no-target 机制, and the affected entity will always be the caster**


## 属性
> *This 机制 has no attributes*


## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - dismount ~onDamaged
```
