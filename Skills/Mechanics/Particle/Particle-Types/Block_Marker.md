## Particle
![img](/Skills/Mechanics/Particle/Particle-Types/images/BLOCK_MARKER.gif)


## Attributes
| Attribute | Aliases   | Description                                                          | Default |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| material | m | 方块数据粒子所基于的材料 | STONE |
> 此粒子具有 [Blockdata](/Skills/Mechanics/Particle/Particle-Types#blockdata) 数据类型，并继承其所有属性



## Examples
```yaml
  Skills:
  - particle{p=block_marker;material=STONE}
```
