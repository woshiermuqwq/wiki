## 描述
Turns the blocks 在target location(s) into falling blocks, making them start to fall as if affected by gravity


## 属性
>*This 技能 has no attributes*  


## 示例
```yml
  Skills:
  - blockdestabilize @BNO{r=5}
```

- [x] destabilizeBlocks
