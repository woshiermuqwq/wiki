## 描述
Makes the player fly, similar to the /fly command. Can use any aura
attribute.


## 属性
> This 机制 inherits every *inheritable* attribute of the [Aura](/Skills/Mechanics/Aura) 机制

  
## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - fly{duration=100} @trigger ~onInteract
```
