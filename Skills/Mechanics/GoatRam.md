## 描述
Causes the casting goat mob to ram the targeted entity

> **This is a [Paper-Only] 机制!**


## 属性
> *This 机制 has no attributes*


## 示例
```yaml
ExampleMob:
  Type: GOAT
  Skills:
  - goatram @trigger ~onInteract
```
