## 描述
Sets 实体的 [pose](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/1.21.6/org/bukkit/entity/Pose.html)


## 属性
| 属性 | 缩写   | 描述                                                          | 默认值 |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| pose      | p         | The pose to assign                                    | <!--type:EntityPose--> |


## 示例
```yaml
  Skills:
  - setpose{pose=DYING}
```
