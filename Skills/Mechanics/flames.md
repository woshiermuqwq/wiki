## 描述
Creates the flames effect 在location of the targeter. Imitates the appearance of Minecraft's mob spawner block flames and thus has a randomness-factor to it.


## 属性
> *This 机制 has no attributes*


## 示例
```yaml
FlamesSkill:
  Skills:
  - flames @self
```


## 别名
- [x] effect:flames
- [x] e:flames
